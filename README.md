# FuturePath AI
### An AI-Powered Internship Matching and Career Readiness Platform

1. Project Overview
FuturePath AI is an AI-powered web platform designed to help university students and fresh graduates find suitable internship opportunities and prepare for their future careers.
The system analyzes students' academic backgrounds, skills, interests, CVs, and career goals to recommend relevant internships. It also provides AI-based CV analysis, skill gap identification, personalized learning roadmaps, and career guidance.
The main goal of FuturePath AI is to connect students with suitable opportunities and help them become more confident and career-ready.

2. Problem Statement
Many university students and fresh graduates face difficulties finding suitable internships and preparing for their future careers. They often lack proper career guidance, professional CVs, and knowledge about the skills required in the job market.
FuturePath AI aims to solve these problems through AI-powered internship matching, CV analysis, skill assessment, and personalized career guidance.

3. Project Objectives
Recommend suitable internships based on students' skills and interests.
Analyze students' CVs and suggest improvements.
Identify missing skills and provide improvement suggestions.
Generate personalized learning roadmaps.
Provide AI-based career guidance.
Help students track their internship applications.
Check internship eligibility.
Support interview preparation.
Suggest suitable job roles and learning resources.
Notify students about upcoming internship deadlines.

##Database Overview (ER Diagram)
The FuturePath AI database is designed to manage student information, internship opportunities, applications, and AI-generated career reports.
The main entities include:
Student – Stores student information, academic details, skills, interests, and career goals.
Skill – Stores skill information required for career development and internship matching.
Employer – Stores employer information, including company name, email, industry type, and website.
Internship Post – Stores internship details, required skills, and application deadlines.
Application – Stores student internship applications, application dates, and progress status.
AI Analysis Report – Stores CV analysis results, match scores, skill gaps, and career readiness scores.
Learning Roadmap – Stores personalized learning goals, learning plans, and progress information.
Learning Resource – Stores recommended learning materials, resource titles, and resource links.
