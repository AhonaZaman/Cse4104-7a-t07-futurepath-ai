# FuturePath AI
### An AI-Powered Internship Matching and Career Readiness Platform

---

## 1. Project Overview

FuturePath AI is an AI-powered web platform designed to help university students and fresh graduates find suitable internship opportunities and prepare for their future careers.

The system analyzes students' academic backgrounds, skills, interests, CVs, and career goals to recommend relevant internships. It also provides AI-based CV analysis, skill gap identification, personalized learning roadmaps, and career guidance.

The main goal of FuturePath AI is to connect students with suitable opportunities and help them become more confident and career-ready.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many university students and fresh graduates face difficulties finding suitable internships and preparing for their future careers. They often lack proper career guidance, professional CVs, and knowledge about the skills required in the job market.

FuturePath AI aims to address these challenges through AI-powered internship matching, CV analysis, skill assessment, and personalized career guidance.

## 3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of FuturePath AI are:

- Recommend suitable internships based on students' skills and interests.
- Analyze students' CVs and suggest improvements.
- Identify skill gaps and recommend ways to improve them.
- Generate personalized learning roadmaps.
- Provide AI-based career guidance.
- Help students track their internship applications.
- Check internship eligibility.
- Support interview preparation.
- Suggest suitable job roles and learning resources.
- Notify students about upcoming internship deadlines.

## 4. Key Features

- **AI-Powered Internship Matching** – Recommends suitable internships based on student profiles.
- **AI-Based CV Analysis** – Analyzes CVs and provides improvement suggestions.
- **Smart Skill Gap Analysis** – Identifies missing skills required for career goals.
- **Personalized Learning Roadmap** – Generates customized learning plans.
- **AI Career Guidance Assistant** – Provides personalized career advice.
- **Internship Application Tracking** – Tracks application progress and status.
- **Internship Eligibility Checker** – Evaluates eligibility for internship opportunities.
- **AI Interview Preparation** – Provides interview questions and preparation tips.
- **Personalized Job Role Suggestions** – Recommends suitable career paths.
- **Learning Resource Recommendations** – Suggests useful courses and tutorials.
- **Internship Deadline Notifications** – Reminds students about upcoming deadlines.

## 5. User Roles

Administrators can:

- Manage Application Deadlines.
- Monitor Platform Activities.

## 6. Database Overview (ER Diagram)

The FuturePath AI database is designed to manage student information, internship opportunities, applications, and AI-generated career reports.

The main database entities include:

1. **Student:** Stores student information, academic details, skills, interests, and career goals.
2. **Skill:** Stores skill information required for career development and internship matching.
3. **Employer:** Stores employer information, including company name, email, industry type, and website.
4. **Internship Post:** Stores internship details, required skills, and application deadlines.
5. **Application:** Stores student internship applications, application dates, and progress status.
6. **AI Analysis Report:** Stores CV analysis results, match scores, skill gaps, and career readiness scores.
7. **Learning Roadmap:** Stores personalized learning goals, learning plans, and progress information.
8. **Learning Resource:** Stores recommended learning materials, resource titles, and resource links.
